Research Overview

My research focuses on NextG Wireless Communication, and I have previously worked on Adaptive Traffic Signal Control and Time Series Forecasting. I apply machine learning and optimization techniques to solve real-world problems in wireless networks, intelligent transportation systems, and financial markets.

Research Areas

1. NextG Wireless Communication

My research in NextG wireless communication focuses on improving network coverage prediction accuracy and resource allocation efficiency. I work with Professor Hyuck M. Kwon at Wichita State University, designing ML-based models for base station placement and failure mitigation strategies, validated across large-scale simulated network scenarios. This work has resulted in improved network coverage prediction accuracy by 15% and enhanced resource allocation efficiency by 20%.

Key Contributions:

  • Paper “NextG Base Station Placement and Failure Mitigation” accepted at IEEE ICNC 2026 (Acceptance Rate: 27%)
  • Designed ML-based models for network optimization
  • Utilized HPC clusters for large-scale model training and deployment

2. Adaptive Traffic Signal Control

I worked on developing intelligent traffic signal control systems using AI agents and zero-shot reasoning. My research involved engineering adaptive traffic signal control agents that could make real-time decisions without task-specific training, significantly reducing intersection wait times.

Key Contributions:

  • Zero Shot Traffic Signal Control project using Llama 3 (via Ollama)
  • Achieved 35% reduction in average intersection wait times in simulation
  • Implemented real-time decision-making without task-specific training

3. Time Series Forecasting

My research in time series forecasting focused on developing advanced neural network models, particularly LSTM-based approaches combined with optimization techniques like Genetic Algorithms (GA) and Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO). This work was conducted under the guidance of Professor Ahmad Esmaeili as part of the course CS 797O: Neural Networks and Deep Learning at Wichita State University. I applied these methods to financial market prediction and other time-dependent problems.

Key Contributions:

  • LSTM-based Stock Market Forecasting with GA and PSO Optimization
  • Achieved 92% directional accuracy in stock trend prediction
  • Poster presented at Kansas Data Science Consortium (KDSC) 2025

Research Collaborations

I work with Professor Hyuck M. Kwon at Wichita State University on NextG Wireless Communication research. My Time Series Forecasting research was conducted under Professor Ahmad Esmaeili’s guidance as part of the course CS 797O: Neural Networks and Deep Learning.
